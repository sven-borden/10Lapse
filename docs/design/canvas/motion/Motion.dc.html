10Lapse 3.0 · Motion

Cinematic when you create, invisible when you work

Choreography plays only when you act: photos arriving, the export running, the finished video. Nothing animates on launch, nothing blocks input, and every move has a reduced-motion version that swaps movement for a short fade. Only transform, opacity, filter and clip-path animate; never layout.
