# 10Lapse privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-19.

10Lapse does not collect, store or transmit personal data.

Your photos and videos are processed on your PC and never leave it. The app has no analytics, no accounts and no network access: the 3.0 package declares no internet capability, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

The app remembers a few settings, such as the last speed, frame shape and export options, and the last import and save folders (the folder paths only, never the photos). They stay on your PC, in the app's own storage, and are removed when you uninstall the app. Settings has a button to reset them.

To keep the preview fast, the app keeps small preview copies of your photos in its own cache on your PC. They never leave your PC, and they are removed when you uninstall the app. The copies are at most 1280 pixels on the longer side, carry no metadata such as the date or location, and a copy the app has not used for 30 days is deleted.

The app also keeps a short log of what it did, for example that an export finished or failed. The log may include the names of files and folders the app worked with, but never the photos themselves, and it is kept for 7 days. It stays on your PC unless you choose to copy it yourself from Settings (Copy diagnostics) to share it. The developer never receives these files from the app.

When an export finishes while the app is in the background, Windows shows a notification that names the video file. It stays on this PC, in the Windows notification center, until you clear it.

If the app crashes, Windows may send a crash report to the developer through its own error reporting. Whether that happens follows your Windows diagnostic data settings, not the app. The report describes the crash and contains no photos or videos.

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/sven-borden/10Lapse/issues.
