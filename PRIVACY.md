# 10Lapse privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-18.

10Lapse does not collect, store or transmit personal data.

Your photos and videos are processed on your PC and never leave it. The app has no analytics, no accounts and no network access: the 3.0 package declares no internet capability, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

The app remembers a few settings, such as your last export options. They stay on your PC, in the app's own storage, and are removed when you uninstall the app.

If the app crashes, Windows may send a crash report to the developer through its own error reporting. Whether that happens follows your Windows diagnostic data settings, not the app. The report describes the crash and contains no photos or videos.

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/sven-borden/10Lapse/issues.
